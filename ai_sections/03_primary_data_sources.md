# Primary Data Sources

## Books
- **Open Library** – Open catalog of books.
- **Project Gutenberg** – Public domain e‑books.
- **Google Books API** – Metadata only (titles, authors, ISBN, etc.).
- **Internet Archive** – Scanned books and texts.
- **Standard Ebooks** – High‑quality formatted e‑books.
- **ManyBooks** – Large collection of free e‑books.
- **HathiTrust** – Academic and research books in the public domain.
- **Digital Public Library of America** – Digitized books from US libraries.

## Research Papers
- **OpenAlex** – Open scholarly graph with papers, authors, institutions.
- **CORE** – Aggregates open‑access research outputs.
- **Semantic Scholar** – AI‑enhanced paper search.
- **arXiv** – Preprints in physics, CS, etc.
- **PubMed** – Biomedical literature.
- **Europe PMC** – Life‑science articles.
- **DOAJ** – Directory of Open Access Journals.
- **BASE** – Academic search engine.
- **SSRN** – Social science research papers.

## Datasets
- **Kaggle (metadata)** – Dataset listings and descriptions.
- **Hugging Face Datasets** – Machine‑learning datasets.
- **Data.gov** – US government open data.
- **Zenodo** – Research data repository.

## Patents
- **Google Patents** – Patent metadata and abstracts.
- **USPTO** – United States patent database.
- **WIPO** – International patent filings.

## Open‑Source Repositories
- **GitHub**, **GitLab**, **SourceForge** – Code repositories and project documentation.

## Government Publications
- **NASA Technical Reports**, **World Bank**, **OECD**, **UNESCO**, **USGS**, **EU Open Data Portal** – Official reports and data sets.

## Documentation
- **MDN**, **Python Docs**, **Java Docs**, **Rust Docs**, **Linux Documentation**, **Kubernetes Docs** – Official language and platform documentation.

These sources are all legally accessible and provide open or public‑domain content.
