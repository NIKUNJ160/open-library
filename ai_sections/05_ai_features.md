# AI Features

## Summarization (AI Summary)
- Generate concise, human‑readable summaries of books, papers, datasets, patents, etc.
- Configurable length (short, medium, long) and tone (formal, casual).

## Explain‑Like‑I’m‑Five (ELI5)
- Produce a simple, jargon‑free explanation of complex concepts.
- Ideal for users new to a domain.

## Key Contributions Extraction
- Identify and list the most important contributions of a research paper (methods, results, impact).
- Output as bullet points with optional citation linking.

## Citation Generation
- Create citations in APA, MLA, Chicago, BibTeX, RIS formats.
- Supports single‑item and batch generation with copy‑to‑clipboard button.

## Question Answering over Documents
- Users can ask natural language questions (e.g., “What method did the authors use for data collection?”) and receive answers extracted from the source.
- Uses context‑aware retrieval‑augmented generation for accuracy.

## Similar Content Recommendation
- Suggest related books, papers, datasets, authors, journals based on semantic similarity and citation networks.
- Includes “You Might Also Like” section on result cards.

## Reading Assistant
- Highlight definitions, equations, and important paragraphs on‑the‑fly.
- Clickable tooltips provide inline explanations or links to external references.

All AI features are optional per user preference and can be toggled in settings.
