# Project Prompt & Objective

**Project Prompt:** Build a Universal Open Knowledge Search Engine

**Objective:**
- Design and build a modern, scalable, AI‑powered search engine that helps users discover legally accessible knowledge resources from across the internet through a single interface.
- The platform should aggregate metadata and links from trusted public and open‑access sources instead of hosting copyrighted content.
- Purpose: simplify discovery of books, research papers, journals, theses, magazines, biographies, educational resources, government publications, technical documentation, and other freely available knowledge.
